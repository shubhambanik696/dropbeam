Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026

DropBeam is built so that we collect nothing. This policy explains, plainly, what that means.

1. We operate no server and store no files

DropBeam is a single web page that runs entirely in your browser. When you share files or text, they travel directly between devices using an encrypted peer-to-peer connection (WebRTC). Your files and messages are never uploaded to, stored on, or seen by DropBeam or its operators. When you close the tab, anything held in the page is gone.

2. Information we do not collect

We do not require accounts, and we do not collect names, email addresses, files, message contents, IP-address logs, analytics, cookies, advertising identifiers, or location data. There is no database.

3. Third-party services

To connect two devices, DropBeam uses a public "signalling" service (PeerJS) that helps browsers find each other. This service only relays the small connection-handshake messages needed to establish a link; it does not receive your files or message contents. Because peer-to-peer connections work by exchanging network addresses, the two devices in a session may become aware of each other's IP addresses — this is inherent to any direct connection and is not recorded by us.

The optional Collect feature uses Google Sign-In and the Google Drive API so that an event organiser can save uploads to their own Google Drive. When you use this feature, your interaction with Google is governed by Google's Privacy Policy. DropBeam requests only the narrow drive.file permission, which limits access to files the app itself creates. Your Google credentials stay in your browser and are never sent to us.

4. Children

DropBeam is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age of digital consent in your country). We do not knowingly collect information from children.

5. Security

Transfers are end-to-end encrypted in transit by the WebRTC protocol. However, no method of transmission is perfectly secure, and you are responsible for the devices and networks you use.

6. Changes

Because DropBeam is open source, this policy may be updated by whoever operates a given deployment. Check this page for the current version.

7. Contact

DropBeam is provided as open-source software. For questions about a specific deployment, contact the operator of the site you are using.
